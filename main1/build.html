How to get an image link

You can host your image on free platforms and copy the direct link:

  • Imgur: Upload here. After uploading, right-click the image → "Copy image address".
  • Postimages: Upload here. Select "Direct Link" after upload.
  • ImageBB: Upload here. Copy the "Direct Link" URL.
  • Other options: TinyPic, ImgBox, Catbox.moe (works for large files).

Make sure your link ends with .png, .jpg, .jpeg, .gif, .webp etc. for it to work.
